1. Program description

Star Hauling Logistics sends text messages to customers, shippers and consignees who request them. Messages may include:

  • Responses and follow-ups to freight quote requests.
  • Pickup and delivery appointment confirmations and reminders.
  • Shipment status updates, including driver ETA, in-transit updates and delivery notifications.
  • Dispatch communications and proof-of-delivery notices.
  • Account and customer service messages, such as replies to your questions.

We do not send marketing messages unless you separately agree to receive them.

2. How you opt in

You opt in by checking the SMS consent box on our quote request form, or by giving us your consent verbally or in writing when you call, text or email our dispatch team. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of doing business with us.
